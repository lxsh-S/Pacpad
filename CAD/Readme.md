# CAD

I've used fusion360 for making the design of the case :) !

## Experience

- This was my first time using a CAD software and the hackpad guide helped me get into this really easily
- The first design I had made was too plain and very boring and I was going to leave it that way but then decided to try to change it a little bit

## Design

- This design came into my mind when I was watching about beaches
- I was trying to make the top case look like a wave and I believe I was somewhat successful in that
- The knob was just stolen from the orpheous pad

## Mistakes and learnings

- lol, I have made too many mistakes that shouldnt be counted here :P
  - One of them was that I forgot to add the screw holes to the case design
  - Then forgot about the distance between the pcb and the ground of the bottom case (had too much space in between). So i tried fixing this issue by adding a hole at the bottom of the case and also reducing the height of the case
  - The design MAY have a flaw, the weird bend that we have at the z-axis(makes the top case look like a wave) may cause an issue during printing. We had a issue of the overhang above the charging port that one of the fellow hackclubber brought to my notice and I removed it. We'll know if the issue is real or not when we print now :)
